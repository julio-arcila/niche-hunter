# Backtest — Gate E, 2026-08-28

Primary result and verdict rule fixed in advance: `reports/backtest_preregistration_2026-08-27.md`.

## Read these three things before any number

1. **Survivorship, and it is the defining limitation rather than a caveat.** Every channel in YouNiverse had crossed 10,000 subscribers by the 2019-10-27 crawl. A channel that was small in 2016 and stayed small was never collected. This measures **relative growth among successes**, never emergence, and no sampling recovers the missing negative class.

2. **The niches were defined by an unvalidated relevance rule.** Held-out precision 0.781 at threshold 0.55, recall 0.694, labelled by the same system that wrote the lexicon, kappa 0.943 against a second model. The human spot-check is deferred to before Slice 7. Every supply number below inherits that error rate.

3. **The backtested `gap` is not the `gap` the live pipeline computes.** `supply.median_views` is not replayable — YouNiverse holds per-video view counts only as of its 2019 crawl, after every decision date here — so `supply.views_per_new_video` stands in for it. The live default is unchanged. A result here transfers to production only as far as that substitution holds.

## Power, before the result

- Niches committed before the data landed: **36**
- Niches surviving selection: **29**
- Smallest rho detectable at this N: **0.378**
- Decision dates: 161 (**7 quasi-independent windows** — the date count is a diagnostic, never a sample size)
- Permutation draws: 10000, niche labels permuted globally

## The primary result

> **FAIL** — p = 0.4988, indistinguishable from the null

Spearman rank correlation between `gap` and `outcome.growth_180d`, over the validation window:

- rho = **0.091**
- permutation p = **0.4988**
- 95% interval: -0.167 to 0.327

### The size baseline

A score that ranks niches by how big they are needs no pipeline to reproduce, so the primary is reported against it rather than alone:

- niche size vs. growth: rho = **-0.019**
- the primary, controlling for size: rho = **0.085**

## Where the ranking goes wrong

Written by hand on 2026-08-28 after the verdict, as `docs/RUNBOOK.md` requires. Everything
below is **exploratory**: none of it was pre-registered, none of it can change the verdict,
and the pre-registration's void clause forbids re-running the primary under different code.

### The instrument worked. That is the uncomfortable part.

The obvious consolations were checked first and none of them hold.

**The score is not flat.** `gap` has sd 0.402 over 4,558 non-null scorecards, spanning
-0.964 to +1.000, with only 2.6% sitting at exactly zero. Slice 3's failure mode — three of
five niches gapping at 0 — did not recur at n=29. The composite discriminates.

**The outcome is not flat either.** This was the hypothesis I expected to confirm and it is
false. Median within-date standard deviation of `growth_180d` is **0.0789** against a mean of
0.211, and the median within-date range is **0.379**. A representative date (2017-05-31,
n=29) runs from 0.082 to 0.530 — a 6.5x spread in log growth. There was ample variance to
rank.

**The test was powered.** 29 of 36 niches survived selection, clearing the pre-registered
floor of 20; the smallest detectable rho at that N is 0.378. This is a null, not an
inconclusive, and the pre-registration is explicit that only the former licenses acting on it.

**Nothing is hiding under niche size.** Size against growth is rho = -0.019, and partialling
size out moves the primary from 0.091 to 0.085. There is no size artifact to strip away.

### Neither input predicts, separately or combined

| series | rho | p | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| `demand` alone | +0.049 | 0.73 | nothing |
| `supply` alone | -0.073 | 0.56 | nothing |
| `gap` (primary) | +0.091 | 0.50 | nothing |

`gap` scoring marginally above both of its components is what pure noise looks like; it is not
evidence that the subtraction helps. **The failure is not in how demand and supply are
combined. It is that neither one carries signal about this outcome.**

### What was actually measured, and what was not

**Zero of 4,517 niche-dates show negative growth.** Not one. Every niche, at every decision
date, grew — between +4.4% and +175% over 180 days. That is survivorship stated as a number
rather than as caveat 1's prose: YouNiverse holds only channels that had crossed 10,000
subscribers by the 2019 crawl, so the corpus contains no failures to rank against successes.

The quantity this gate tested is therefore **relative growth rate among channels that had
already succeeded.** The product's claim is a different one — that the scorecard finds niches
where a *newcomer* can emerge. **That claim was not tested, and it cannot be tested on this
corpus**, because the channels that stayed small were never crawled and no sampling recovers
them. A null on the first says little about the second, in either direction.

This is the honest reading, and it is not a rescue: the gate answered the question it was
built to answer, and the answer was no. But the question it answered is narrower than the one
the roadmap cares about, and that narrowing was known and recorded in advance rather than
discovered here.

### Known defects in this run that limit it further

1. **Openness was absent from the backtest entirely, through an error in the metric set.**
   `winner_age_years` and `top10_concentration` were listed in `replay.BACKTEST_METRICS` and
   returned NULL for all 5,568 cluster-days, because `video_snapshots` holds **0 rows** in
   `data/backtest.db` — a deliberate load decision that `nh/backtest/load.py`'s own docstring
   states, and that the metric-set comment then contradicted by claiming `winner_age_years`
   "needs only channel creation dates and video views on niche". Neither feeds `gap`, so the
   primary is unaffected, but 11,136 feature rows and roughly a fifth of the replay's compute
   produced nothing, and no openness signal was ever in evidence.
2. **The supply side rests on a thin decided fraction.** `on_niche_share` averages confidence
   0.106 across the replay; a sampled cluster-day decided 350 of 5,370 videos and left 5,020
   unscorable or undecided. Caveat 2's 0.781 precision is applied to a minority of the corpus,
   and the majority is silent rather than negative.
3. **`uploads_per_week` averages confidence 0.072** with 16.6% NULL ("cluster has no member
   channels"). It is not a primary input, but it is one of the two supply proxies the
   pre-registration named, and it is in no condition to serve as the secondary comparison.
4. **The substitution stands unquantified.** Caveat 3 notes the backtested `gap` is built on
   `views_per_new_video` rather than `median_views`. Nothing here measures how far apart those
   two rankings are, so the transfer of this null to the live pipeline is asserted, not
   measured.

### What follows

The roadmap's pre-committed consequence applies: **do not build the dashboard.** Of its two
branches, the evidence points away from "return to the feature work and try again on this
corpus" — the corpus cannot express emergence, so a better feature computed against it would
be a better answer to the wrong question. The remaining branch is to narrow the claim to
*surfaces evidence for a human to judge*, which is a claim this null does not contradict.

If the predictive claim is to be revived it needs a different instrument, not a different
threshold: a corpus containing channels that failed. Nothing in `nh/` needs to change for that
and nothing here licenses changing it.

**Not to be done:** re-running the primary with another stratum, threshold, horizon or supply
proxy in search of a better number. The pre-registration voids on exactly that, and a
secondary that wins after the fact is a hypothesis for a later slice with a fresh validation
window.
